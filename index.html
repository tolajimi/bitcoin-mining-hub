What is Digital Bitcoin Mining?

A short, plain-language overview of the model.

⚡

Real hashrate share

Digital miners typically represent a claim on actual computing power running on professional ASICs. You receive a portion of the Bitcoin rewards generated by that hashrate.

🖥️

No personal hardware

You do not buy, host, or maintain machines. Electricity, cooling, and operations are handled by the platform. Your main costs are the miner purchase and ongoing maintenance fees.

🔄

Upgradeable & transferable

Many platforms let you increase power or efficiency later and offer a secondary market so you can sell or trade your digital miner if your plans change.
